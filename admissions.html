SHOMS ADMISSION

STAGE ONE: The first stage is through a competitive entrance examination. This is normally open to all without
any bias for sex, religion or ethnic background, as long as a child make the requisite performance in the
entrance examination. The entrance examination is in three subjects areas:

USE OF ENGLISH and
MATHEMATICS
Candidates are expected to score the cut of mark set for the examination. which is 50%

STAGE TWO: Oral Interview: At this stage, the confidence level of the child is tested. The educational background of the child is also determined.

WELCOME TO SHOMS: After passing the stages above your child can now be ushered into our school as a Full time pupil. A matriculation induction ceremony will be held for a newly admitted students.
